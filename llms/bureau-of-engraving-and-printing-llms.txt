# Bureau of Engraving and Printing

> The Bureau of Engraving and Printing (BEP) is a manufacturing bureau of the U.S.
> Department of the Treasury. It designs and prints Federal Reserve notes and other
> U.S. government security documents at facilities in Washington, DC and Fort Worth,
> Texas. BEP does not publish an API, an SDK, a developer portal, or any
> machine-readable specification. This file was generated by API Evangelist from the
> public bep.gov site as a third-party profile; BEP does not serve an llms.txt of its
> own (https://www.bep.gov/llms.txt returns 404).

## What BEP publishes for machines

Nothing. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection, JSON Schema,
MCP server, A2A agent card, `/.well-known/` document or `llms.txt` on any BEP-controlled
host (www.bep.gov, bep.gov, moneyfactory.gov). Currency production figures are published
as HTML tables only — no CSV, JSON or XML download. BEP has no GitHub organization and
no organization page on data.gov.

## Public services (human-facing, free, non-programmatic)

- [U.S. Currency Reader Program](https://www.bep.gov/services/currency-accessibility/us-currency-reader-program): Free handheld currency readers for blind and visually impaired U.S. residents. Requested by mailed application form, not by API.
- [U.S. Currency Reader Program FAQs](https://www.bep.gov/services/currency-accessibility/us-currency-reader-program/us-currency-reader-program-faqs): Eligibility, certification and delivery questions.
- [Currency Reader Application Form](https://www.bep.gov/services/currency-accessibility/us-currency-reader-program/us-currency-reader-application-form): The paper/PDF application.
- [Mutilated Currency Redemption](https://www.bep.gov/services/mutilated-currency-redemption): Free redemption of severely damaged Federal Reserve notes. Claims are submitted by shipping the notes to BEP's Mutilated Currency Division.
- [Mutilated Currency FAQs](https://www.bep.gov/services/mutilated-currency-redemption/mutilated-currency-faqs): What qualifies, and how long a claim takes.
- [Packing Directions](https://www.bep.gov/services/mutilated-currency-redemption/packing-directions): How to package a mutilated currency claim.
- [Meaningful Access Program](https://www.bep.gov/services/currency-accessibility/meaningful-access-program): BEP's program for making U.S. currency accessible.

## Mobile applications (end-user, not SDKs)

- [Mobile Applications](https://www.bep.gov/services/currency-accessibility/mobile-applications): EyeNote for iOS and Android — free BEP-developed apps that denominate Federal Reserve notes from a phone camera. They identify denomination only and do not authenticate notes. Distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; there is no public SDK, package registry entry or API behind them.

## Notes for agents

- There is no endpoint to call. Any agent task involving BEP resolves to reading
  www.bep.gov or to a postal-mail workflow operated by a human.
- Currency *education* material at uscurrency.gov is published by the U.S. Currency
  Education Program (Federal Reserve Board), not by BEP — do not attribute it here.
- moneyfactory.gov is a legacy BEP domain that 302-redirects to www.bep.gov.
- Coin production is the U.S. Mint, a different Treasury bureau. Federal payments and
  fiscal data APIs are the Bureau of the Fiscal Service, also a different bureau.
